Feature name: Editor page controls on the page side

What I want: Put the page controls (zoom in, zoom out, reset zoom,
paper settings, previous/next page) in a small vertical icon bar on the
right edge of the page. Not hidden inside the three-dots menu.

Why (the problem): Now I must open the three-dots menu every time I want to
zoom or change page. It is slow.

How it should work: The icons are always visible next to the page.
One tap does the action. The bottom toolbar stays the same.

Where in the app: Editor screen, right side of the page.

What should NOT change: The bottom floating toolbar. The drawing and
text tools. The saved notes.

Edge cases / worries: Small phone screens, landscape, the bar covering the
page, the bar blocking drawing near the right edge. Not sure what to do
with the three-dots menu: remove it or keep it for some actions?

Priority: not sure
